# Effect: Blast Boots (Major)
![[systems-pf2e-icons-equipment-worn-items-other-worn-items-blast-boots.webp|150 lp right]]

For 1 minute, the _major blast boots_ remain active, allowing you to clumsily fly through the air for the duration. You gain a 30-foot Fly speed for the duration, but any time you [[Fly]], you are [[Clumsy 1]] until the start of your next turn.
