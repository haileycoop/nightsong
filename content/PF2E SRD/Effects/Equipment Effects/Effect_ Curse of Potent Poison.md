# Effect: Curse of Potent Poison
![[systems-pf2e-icons-hazards-serpent-ward-curse-of-potent-poison.webp|150 lp right]]

This curse magically speeds poison through your body.

* * *

**Effect** You take a -1 status penalty to Fortitude saves and you gain weakness 3 to poison (if you already have weakness to poison, increase it by 3). The curse remains until removed by _[[Cleanse Affliction]]_ or similar magic.
