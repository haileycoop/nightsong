# Effect: Beastmaster's Sigil - Ranged Weapon (Greater)
![[systems-pf2e-icons-default-icons-effect.svg|150]]

Granted by [[Beastmaster's Sigil (Greater)]]

If you critically hit with the affixed weapon, and the target is adjacent to a creature summoned with the _sigil_, the target takes 2d6 bleed{2d6 persistent bleed} damage.
