# Effect: Glittering Snare (Failure)
![[systems-pf2e-icons-equipment-snares-glittering-snare-effect.webp|150 lp right]]

The creature takes a -2 status penalty to Stealth checks for 2 minutes or until it spends a 2-action activity, which has the manipulate trait, to remove the glitter. If the creature was [[Invisible]], the glitter outlines it for 1 round, making it [[Concealed]] rather than [[Undetected]] to visual senses.
