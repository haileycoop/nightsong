### Summary

- When the settlers arrived there were two separate continents, a northern and southern continent
- The settlers found it easiest to survive and defend with their backs to a mountain range, not far from the coast
- They succeeded in founding a number of cities, and creating a coordinated defense against the assaults of Ondra
- That was until Ondra literally smashed the southern continent into the northern
- This created a second mountain range, and the ruins of the civilisation were contained within a triangular area surrounded by mountains, known today as [[The Maw]]
- The area south of this new mountain range was shattered due to the force of the impact and is known as [[The Jagged Wastes]]
