Nightsong is an original setting for tabletop role-playing games, specifically campaign-based heroic games like Pathfinder (similar to D&D). But maybe it will have 

Currently, I (Hailey) am treating it as my own project and I am the sole writer for this Worldbuilding portal. However, I have been using some collaborative worldbuilding processes with friends to develop the ideas. So I'm not claiming sole authorship. 

But, I do a lot of collaboration, and frankly it's nice to have a solo creative project where I call the shots. It's a personal hobby art project, and I have no designs on publishing or whatever. I am determined to avoid my pattern of turning a hobby into a hustle.

And, I'm keen for people to develop ideas with, and that's why I made this portal. If you are seeing it, you probably know me already, and I'm probably already developing these ideas with you.

The content and themes of Nightsong are based on some ideas I've been bouncing around for a while. Originally I wanted to run a campaign for my friend Bec and I asked her what kind of stuff she likes in a world. She likes lots of little spirits to discover like stumbling across [Hestu](https://zelda.fandom.com/wiki/Hestu) in Breath of the Wild. That campaign never happened, but I did some early sketches back then. I was also bringing in some sci-fi ideas, influenced in particular by [The Algebraist](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/12009.The_Algebraist) by Iain M. Banks.

More recently, my forever GM friend Abigail asked me to run a short Pathfinder 2 campaign for her. I took it as an opportunity to develop out these ideas further. This campaign is now underway, and I will be taking ideas that emerge from the campaign as the primary worldbuilding input for this setting. The [[content/index|player portal]] for this campaign is a great intro to the setting.

At the same time, I will be working with another set of friends who are interested in the worldbuilding side of things. I will use games like Microscope, Follow, i'm sorry did you say street magic, and more, to build out the setting.

If you are here, you're in that second group. Let's make a world!
