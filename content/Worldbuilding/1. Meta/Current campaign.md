#### Notes on how to GM

Just 3 hexes https://chgowiz-games.blogspot.com/2017/11/just-three-hexes-campaign-starters.html

Re-watch and take notes: [How to create a Sandbox Campaign](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mj94BYvm3kU)

Sandbox
- have themes to explore
- have factions and conflicts ready to be set into motion by the players
- players must be proactive
- need good encounter tables

Encounters
- Encourage players to ask questions
- UNAT (esp exploration, puzzle)
	- Understand - checks to get the lay of the land
	- Navigate - obstacles and dangerous terrain to make it hard to reach the puzzle
	- Advance - roll to succeed at progressing the puzzle
	- Threat - some threat that gets closer, or progressively damages characters
- Consequences for bad rolls

#### Start - the entrance to The Maw

- There's a town that guards the passage into the maw
- It's full of adventurers and supplies but it's also run by some governor
- Intrigue could happen there
- But broadly the players are going into the maw to seek treasure or some other quest they have from their backstory
- There may be a fee just to enter the maw

#### Save this for later - Eldergrove
- the players all need to have some reason to be in Eldergrove
	- they just think there's treasure in the ruins
	- they live in Eldergrove and grew up there
	- a scholar sent them on a knowledge quest
	- they are seeking rare monster parts
	- they are fleeing trouble elsewhere and this place is remote
	- they need to get to the deepwilds

- not long after they arrive, a corrupted dungeon goat breaks out, and they battle it
	- the elder has his people stand back to give the newcomers a chance to prove themselves
	- after they defeat this enemy they are allowed to head into the depths

- some secret in the depths is revealed that leads to a future story

- deeper in the ruins, there are secrets that would reveal much of the origin story of the world, and insights that would allow the emerging scientists of the current age to make big leaps. but that should be out of reach at this stage

#### General notes & ideas on life today

- Species with darkvision that live together with other species should be more nocturnal and take the "night shift" of society
- This works out as these civilisations need to be on constant guard against threat
- Species with darkvision should have some issues in full daylight
- I want an economy around monster parts as valuable ingredients for elixirs and magical reagents, so that hunting monsters is desirable
	- Check out Monsters of Drakkenheim for rules on monster parts
