### ideas to build out the Maw

Hex ideas / rumours in town

- A witch that makes elixirs out of monster parts and will trade for monster parts. Has some huge intelligent monster pet (or maybe the monster is the witch and the humanoid is the protector)
- A necromancer with a tower. Since Ondra makes echos out of unsanctified dead, they have profaned their whole hex and filled it with death traps. PCs can’t be resurrected and won’t go to their god if they die there.
- Echoes attacking the mining camp

Factions / People
- Eldergrove - might encounter a ranger early
- Harmony council
- The Escape
- The bandits that have a secret passage into the maw
- Dragon cult (Kobolds)
- Monster hunter cult? Monster tamers? Actually live in the Maw
- The hermit

Prep
- Create a palette of monsters that are in or out
- Create lots of random gen tables for encounters and locations
- Use existing tables to generate my locations, but edit them to fit with my desired flavour
- Start noting down my themes and vibes around the world and the Maw so I know how to filter

Other ideas
- Echos - undead that Ondra creates - even if you kill it, the Ondra has recorded the spirit, and keeps re-constructing it based on the original person
	- Related to the idea that Ondra reflects your being as a way to fight you

### what's outside the Maw

In the green area south of the jagged wastelands, I imagine that's where there is some level of civilisation. But because Ondra is so vicious, it's a patchwork of lesser kingdoms and city states. There is much unclaimed wild territory even there.
