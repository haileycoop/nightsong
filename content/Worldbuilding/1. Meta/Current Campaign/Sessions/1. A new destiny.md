### Initial encounter 

#### Setup 

- It's the evening and some of the non-Ration camp dwellers are gathered around the fire
- There's a bit of a drum circle
- There's a shaggy druid person playing an interesting rhythm
- That's when a [[razorworm]] burst forth up through the middle of the fire
- It's now dark
- The raiders have horses or similar waiting outside the camp so they can get away with spoils
- Their real target is the Origin

During the encounter
- Raiders are trying to quickly get away with stuff
- They are also doing things to add to the chaos like setting fires
- Add some weapons or something maybe ballistae they can turn on it
- The shaggy druid can use the drum as an action to either draw the monster towards it or away from it
- It can also tell the monster to flee which it will do rather than let the monster die
- If the shaggy druid dies, the monster fights to the death
- Every time they get additional help, the clock fills
	- Raiders get food
	- Raiders get weapons
	- Raiders get plans
	- Raiders get the origin
