Some of the deepwild peoples returned to explore the ruins of the bastion. What they found there resonated with cultural memory of their time among the stars. They were able to take away a sense of the desire to escape, and a few clues about fundamental science. 

They came to see Ondra as a prison from which their ancestors were trying to escape. They believe Var is their jailor, and the Nightsong is a kind of brainwashing to keep them docile.

Their mild technological advances allowed them to thrive in the deepwilds, and they are now branching out, trying to influence events in other centers of power. Their ultimate goal is to escape Ondra and even destroy Var.
