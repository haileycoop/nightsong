### Summary
*`= this.oneliner`*

C.L. = Caster Lemma = the words they use for defining their camps, which are kind of like ships
	- But they use "xe/xyr" instead of "she/her"
	- They names combine a geometry term and a virtue
	- this one is "C.L. Patient Ordinal" (ordinal is the Y axis and this is the furthest north camp)
