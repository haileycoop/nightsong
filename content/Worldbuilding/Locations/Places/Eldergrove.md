### Guardianship of the Bastion

With Eldergrove's inhabitants maintaining a careful balance between civilization and nature, treasure hunters are both discouraged and occasionally permitted, though often stirring the depths' dormant dangers.

All of the easy pickings are gone anyway, and the people of Eldergrove have colonised the upper levels. They have fungus farms, and dungeon goats help keep the wild deepflora in check.

Treasure hunters are blocked and discouraged, but sometimes there is reason to allow them in.

### Culture

This is the tale of your home, nestled within a cradle of low mountains and rolling hills, blanketed by the densest of forest canopies. It's here, amidst nature's quietude, where the morning sun bestows its first gentle kiss, before yielding to the shadows cast by the mountains as night falls.

Your village, Eldergrove, was founded upon the ruins of a civilization lost to time - its forgotten technologies and arcane magics are scavenged to support our strange community’s coexistence. But remember, coexistence does not imply tranquility. Life in Eldergrove is as much about the struggle and discord that arises from different beings trying to find their place as it is about the peace we find in shared purpose.

The inhabitants of Eldergrove are as diverse as the flora that blankets the forest floor. Humans, elves, goblins, orcs, bugbears - all have found their way to this sanctuary. Many, just like you, were misfits elsewhere, bearing stories etched in their hearts, seeking acceptance, or perhaps simply escape. Eldergrove is elusive, but to those who truly need it, it seems to reveal itself.

You'll find skilled craftsmen shaping nature into art, herbalists coaxing secrets from plants, and healers who see remedies in the forest's bounty. There are hunters who heed the call of the wild, and young apprentices eager to carve out their identity in this intricate tapestry. We are governed by a council of elders, who carry the weight of decision making, not always easy and not always met with unanimous approval. Yet, we muddle through.

The village offers wooden houses crowned with thatched roofs, a square that has seen countless sunrises and sunsets, a communal hall echoing with shared joys and sorrows. Eldergrove boasts a bountiful greenhouse, a workshop alive with creativity, a library whispering ancient wisdom, and a temple that sings the hymns of the old forest gods. Streams flowing from the surrounding mountains quench our thirst, their crystal waters collected diligently.

You might have been an Eldergrove dweller for only a season, or perhaps your feet have grown roots here, your heart echoing the forest's rhythm. Regardless, you are an integral part of Eldergrove. Its struggles are yours, and so are its victories. The world beyond our forest may not understand or accept our way of life, but know that within these woods, we shape our future together, one day at a time. Remember, every challenge is but a chance for growth. After all, even the grandest tree in Eldergrove was once just a seed.

#### Creating your character

Now, kin of Eldergrove, the time has come for you to breathe life into your new personas, inhabitants of Eldergrove.

Keep in mind that these characters you craft are fresh to the world, mere Level 1 residents of our unique village. But fret not about the details of skills, classes or abilities for now. We're going to explore something even more vital - their heart and soul.

When crafting your character, I urge you to dive deeper. What ignites their passion? What fuels their dreams under the moonlit forest canopy? Are they a quiet herbalist, finding peace in the rustling leaves and whispering winds, or a restless young apprentice eager to master the forge’s dance of fire and metal? Perhaps they are a burly orc, who despite his intimidating facade, is a gentle guardian of Eldergrove's children? What are their fears, their weaknesses, their personal trials?

To aid you in this journey, consider these prompts:

- What would your character sacrifice everything for?
- What secret does your character hold that they fear might come to light?
- Who in Eldergrove does your character care most about and why?
- What does your character regret, and how does this influence their present?
- What simple pleasure does your character cherish above all else?
