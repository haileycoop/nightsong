### Summary
*`= this.oneliner`*

This town guards the pass into [[The Maw]]

Its name pays homage to Durinson, a legendary figure or ancient faction that played a significant role in the town's founding or early history. It adds a sense of nostalgia and reverence to the town's identity, suggesting a connection to its past while also emphasizing its function as a place of respite for weary adventurers.

It is governed by a factional called the "Harmony Council." This name reflects their outward appearance of maintaining order and balance within Durinson's Rest. Led by a charismatic leader known as "Eldon Grey," the Harmony Council presents itself as a benevolent governing body, promoting the idea that their regulations and taxes are necessary to ensure the safety and success of adventurers venturing into The Maw. However, beneath this facade of harmony lies a complex web of power struggles, corruption, and hidden agendas, with Eldon Grey pulling the strings to maintain control and profit from the town's lucrative ventures.
