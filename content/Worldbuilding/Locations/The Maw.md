### Summary

- Created by [[The Crush]]
- Contains the ruins of the first settler civilisation
- Now overgrown with thick jungle and swamps
- Contains [[The Bastion]]
- Only known way in is through a southern pass which is guarded by a settlement that controls the pass, and extracts value from adventurers
- The only known way in is [[Durinson's Rest]]
- There is a bandit faction that has a way under the mountains west of the pass, and they rove within the Maw seeking treasure or ambushing adventurers
