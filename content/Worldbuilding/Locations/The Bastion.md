### Summary

During the period of he Bastion emerged as a collaborative effort, drawing upon the strengths of various races to create a beacon of hope amidst Ondra's challenges. It was a place to eventually rebuild the basis for a technological society, over the course of many generations.

### History

#### Creation

The Dwarves carved it out of the bare rock. The gnomes came up with clever ways to preserve knowledge and tech carving fundamental science into its very walls. The goblins accelerated the works through early machines. The orcs learned to live in harmony with the lands around the bastion. The halfling's translated the Orc's bounty into healthy food. The humans ranged greater distances, and kept up relations with other settlements. And the elves ensured that there was culture and not just a mission, keeping the place lively.

#### Rise

Initially the Bastion flourished as a hub of trade and learning, despite its remote location. Its decline came with the depletion of technology and the emergence of new generations with divergent dreams.

#### Fall

As the centuries past, and the last of the tech withered, the hope was lost. New generations developed new native dreams, and even began to doubt the old stories. The Bastion instead became a center of power, and a tantalising prize for emerging conquerers.

After changing hands a few times, all its cultural coherence was lost, and because of its remote location, it fell. Some cataclysm meant it was abandoned, and soon the wilds overran it. But not before it had grown to be many levels deep, with considerable defences around it.

Some of the inhabitants who had grown used to living underground, fled deeper into natural caverns and learned to live in the deepwilds there. These people would be this world's equivalent of underdark humanoid species.

#### Abandonment and Ruin

Amidst political turmoil and cataclysmic events, the Bastion was ultimately abandoned, reclaimed by the wilds, and left to crumble into ruin.

### Eldergrove: Guardian of the Ruins

#### Legacy

Eventually, people returned to the ruins, to scavenge its depths for treasure, and sometimes knowledge. It was so wealthy that a town grew up around it.

[[Eldergrove]], a town born from the ruins of the Bastion, embraces a radical culture in harmony with the wilds, controlling access to the depths below.

#### Guardianship
