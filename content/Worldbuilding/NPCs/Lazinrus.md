An NPC from Rufus's backstory

> Rufus was left as an infant on the altar to the Adamant Ratio. Minotaurs are unheard of in the city and are considered either a curse or a blessing. The temple took him in and raised him. 
> His mentor is an older human by the name of Lazinrus. Rufus adores him and craves his praise. Rufus learned early on that he was praised for his strength and leaned into the martial side of society.
