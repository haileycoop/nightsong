### Settlement Stories on Ondra

#### [[Elves]]

The elves aimed to create a serene and sometimes decadent haven but depleted their resources quickly, leading to struggles to maintain their ideal society.
- [[Elves]] from some too easy, too lovely place

#### [[Dwarves]]

Determined to establish a stronghold, the dwarves carved their settlement out of bare rock, focusing on durability and defense.
- [[Dwarves]] from a large rocky planet with heavy gravity and a hostile surface

#### Orcs

Wisely, the orcs prioritized understanding the land, conserving their technology until necessary, and adapting to coexist with nature.
- [[Orcs]] - similar origin to humans

#### [[Humans]]

Exploratory by nature, humans scattered across Ondra, seeking viable settlements but often intermingling with other species.

#### Gnomes

Crafting secretive plans, gnomes preserved knowledge and technology meticulously, living austere lives in service of a return to the stars.
- [[Gnomes]] - Also from a planet with a hostile surface but lesser gravity than dwarves

#### Halflings

Thrifty and stealthy, halflings tried to create a traditional, peaceful agrictulural settlement. Like the elves, their failure to adapt to the harsh realities of Ondra left them struggling.

#### Goblins

Utilizing available materials efficiently, goblins swiftly advanced from the Stone Age, embracing innovation to propel their society forward.
