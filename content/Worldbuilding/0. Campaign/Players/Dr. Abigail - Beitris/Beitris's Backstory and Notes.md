# Relic

Made of brass, when worn they are contiguous brass with no visible seams, when not worn they merge and morph into a cube. 

When a gemstone, crystal, or aeon/ioun stone is pressed against one of them by the wearer, the brass of the bracers will form around the stone holding it in place.  As a single action the wearer can motion the brass structure of the bracers to morph in a way to cover or uncover the stone as needed. A covered aeon stone is temporarily suppressed. Only one such stone can be held by the bracers at a time, on either arm.  Attempting to hold another stone on either of the bracers will make the former stone fall out.

The shifting, morphing nature of the bracers responds to the wearer intuitively, providing a +1 item bonus to Acrobatics checks and a +1 item bonus to your Reflex DC against Trip attempts.

The Bracers have the Minor Gift of Enhancing Circuitry (RoE pg148)

# Backstory

Beitris (Tìr-Majorie)

She was found as a small child of 5 years old, in a stasis like trance inside a cracked open geode made of brass and iron, during an earthquake.  Not remembering anything of her past, she was taken in by a tribe of nomadic barbarian people and given her secret “father name” "Tìr-Majorie" which means "Pearl of the Earth" in their language, until her mother name could be found.

While growing up Beitris was simultaneously a source of pure joy and complete mischief.  Always smiling and bringing happiness to those around her, and getting into the worst sort of trouble with her friends, her curiosity seemed to know no bounds.  She was incapable of sitting still for longer than 3 seconds and was unable to stay quiet for an even shorter time.

She was given the mother name Beitris, which in her native language means "bringer of joy and happiness".  She was also found to be wise beyond her short years, an old soul or ban-fhiosaiche (wise woman) as her people say. Her emotional intuitiveness and wisdom led her to make many friends and become something of a social glue for her peers.

As she grew into adolescence she displayed amazing powers that none in her tribe possessed, even those among the uchd (people adopted, found, or taken in). She could move things with her mind, and sometimes displayed other psychic abilities like being able to hear people’s emotions or surface thoughts.  This drew understandable suspicion from people in her tribe, and if she were any other person she may even have become a pariah, but dang she was such a cheerful and happy person that they tolerated her, especially her close friends and family.  Her natural sense of duty, combined with her naivety, led her to use her powers for good instead of harm, plus or minus the odd prank.  The thought of using her gifts to harm others never crossed her mind until she needed to use them in self defence, and even then it was only against unthinking beasts and for hunting.

It was when her psychic powers manifested when the visions started.  It often had a different underlying background, but always the same theme.  
It would feature a whale (whatever that was, not many of those in the highlands) swimming amongst a backdrop of what she thought was a vast ocean, but then she realised it was the stars themselves.  The whale then would turn into a great beast with legs that walked on land.  
She had no idea where it came from or what it represented, but it felt to her like something or someone calling to her.  It came to her during the nightsong, when the world was at rest and her mind was most clear.  Over time though it started creeping more and more into her every day mind, until she learned to keep it under control. When she came of age and was permitted to wander beyond the sights of her tribe, her curiosity took her "beyond the next ridge", or "around the next ravine" one too many times.  Sometimes she wondered if she was searching for the source of her visions, but really she just wanted to see things she hadn't seen before.  She found wonderous things that tried to kill her and her friends, and sometimes inanimate things that also tried to kill her and her friends.  Once in a while her travels in the highland badlands that her tribe called home would produce odd artefacts of strange origins that hinted at such a vast world outside the scope of her knowledge that it stoked the embers of her curiosity to travel the world.

Her adventures while exploring led her to encounter a Travelling College caravan pulled by large wombats, creatures that looked like those from her visions.  It was a revelation! She packed her things, said her goodbyes, and went with the caravan.  She absorbed as much knowledge as she could from these people, and was given an almanack of knowledge of the outside world which she has read and re-read several times over.
 
Life in the Jagged Wastes was difficult and harsh.  Food, while scarce, could be found.  The challenge for survival was in shelter and protection from the elements, and from beasts and nature itself.  By the time that Beitris is ready for more adventure she is already an accomplished adventurer.  She has strong survival skills, prestigious mental powers, and most importantly, a plucky attitude.
