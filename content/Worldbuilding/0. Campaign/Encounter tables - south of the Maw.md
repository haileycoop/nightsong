When players are on their way to [[Durinson's Rest]] , I will be usin "hexploration" rules and random tables to create their sessions.

These tables, while random, should be customised to express the world of Ondra. A random encounter should at minimum enrich the feeling of being in this world.

But more so, it should provide a clue about what a faction is doing, a fragment of lore connecting to deeper mysteries, a hook for side quest, or an opportunity for character development.

At the start of each adventuring day, my plan is to roll 3 times on the Master Table. I will then piece together these 3 elements in an interesting way.

#### Master table
If they stay on the road, they are less likely to run into trouble. Any special locations on the road will be a 100% chance of encounter.

##### Monsters - on the road
1.  **Bandit Gang**
    - Description: A group of human bandits armed with swords and bows, looking to ambush and rob travelers.
    - Numbers: 1d4+1 bandits (Bandit, Bestiary 1, page 24)
    - Interaction: They can be reasoned with or bribed, but if negotiations fail, they will resort to violence.
2. **Hunting Drake**   
    - Description: A solitary drake, hunting for food and fiercely territorial.
    - Numbers: 1 hunting drake (Drake, Bestiary 1, page 132)
    - Interaction: The drake is aggressive but can be driven off with a show of force or a sufficient bribe of food.
3. **Giant Wolves**
    - Description: A pack of large, intelligent wolves, hunting for prey.
    - Numbers: 1d4+2 giant wolves (Wolf, Bestiary 1, page 358)
    - Interaction: The wolves are primarily interested in food and will avoid a fight if they sense a strong threat. Offering food might appease them.
4. **Barbarian Patrol**
    - Description: A patrol of barbarian warriors and clan members, possibly scouting the area or on a mission for their tribe.
    - Numbers: 2d12+2 barabarians
    - Interaction: The group can be negotiated with if approached with respect or if offered a trade. They may also challenge the party for dominance.
5. **Boggard Ambush** 
    - Description: A group of boggards lying in wait to ambush unsuspecting travelers.
    - Numbers: 1d4+2 boggards (Boggard, Bestiary 1, page 36)
    - Interaction: Boggards are more likely to attack on sight, but they may be open to negotiation if offered something of value.
6. **Dire Boar**
    - Description: A large, aggressive boar, defending its territory or searching for food.
    - Numbers: 1 dire boar (Boar, Bestiary 1, page 52)
    - Interaction: The dire boar is more likely to attack if threatened but can be driven away with a show of force or loud noises.

##### Monsters - off the road
1. **Hill Giants**
    - Description: A pair of hill giants causing havoc in the area, possibly looking for food or entertainment.
    - Numbers: 2 hill giants (Hill Giant, Bestiary 1, page 184)
    - Interaction: They can be reasoned with, though they are more likely to demand tribute in the form of food or valuables.
2. **Chimera**
    - Description: A fearsome chimera prowling the wilderness, searching for prey to sate its hunger.
    - Numbers: 1 chimera (Chimera, Bestiary 1, page 68)
    - Interaction: The chimera is aggressive and difficult to reason with, but might be distracted with a sufficient offering of meat.
3. **Wyvern Nest**
    - Description: A pair of wyverns nesting in the area, defending their territory fiercely.
    - Numbers: 1d2+1 wyverns (Wyvern, Bestiary 1, page 332)
    - Interaction: They are highly territorial and will attack intruders on sight, but might be avoided with stealth or distraction.
4. **Troll Ambush**
    - Description: A group of trolls lying in wait for unwary travelers, seeking to feast on flesh.
    - Numbers: 1d4 trolls (Troll, Bestiary 1, page 320)
    - Interaction: Trolls are difficult to reason with, driven primarily by hunger. Fire or acid might deter them.
5. **Boggard Swarm**
    - Description: A larger group of boggards, possibly including a boggard priest, set on ambushing intruders.
    - Numbers: 1d4+4 boggards, and 1 boggard priest (Boggard, Bestiary 1, page 36; Boggard Priest, Bestiary 1, page 37)
    - Interaction: They are more organized and can be negotiated with, though their demands may be high.
6. **Gorgon**
    - Description: A terrifying gorgon wandering the area, turning creatures to stone.
    - Numbers: 1 gorgon (Gorgon, Bestiary 1, page 170)
    - Interaction: Highly aggressive and territorial, the gorgon will attack on sight. It is nearly impossible to reason with, but might be lured away with a clever trap or distraction.



#### Travel obstacles
1. **Rockslides**
    - Description: A sudden rockslide blocks the path, making travel dangerous and slow.
    - Challenge: Characters must make Athletics or Acrobatics checks to navigate the debris. Failure results in potential damage or being pinned.
2. **Swift River Crossing** 
    - Description: A swollen river from recent rains is too deep and fast to wade through safely.
    - Challenge: Characters must find a way to cross, using Survival or Athletics checks to build a temporary bridge, find a ford, or use ropes. Failure could result in being swept downstream.
3. **Mudslides** 
    - Description: Heavy rains cause the ground to become unstable, resulting in a mudslide.
    - Challenge: Characters must make Reflex saves to avoid being caught in the slide. Those caught must make Strength checks to free themselves.
4. **Narrow Ledge** 
    - Description: The path narrows to a precarious ledge with a steep drop on one side.
    - Challenge: Characters must make Acrobatics checks to traverse the ledge safely. Failure results in slipping, possibly falling, and taking damage.
5. **Fallen Tree**
    - Description: A large tree has fallen across the path, blocking the way.
    - Challenge: Characters must make Athletics checks to climb over or work together to clear the tree. Alternatively, they could find a way around, which may add extra time to their journey.
6. **Overgrown Path**
    - Description: Thick underbrush and vines have overtaken the path, making progress slow and difficult.
    - Challenge: Characters must use Survival or Nature checks to clear the way efficiently. Failure results in slowed travel and possible encounters with hidden creatures.
7. **Steep Ascent**
    - Description: A particularly steep section of the path requires careful climbing.
    - Challenge: Characters must make Athletics checks to climb the steep incline. Failure results in falling and taking damage.
8. **Hidden Sinkhole** 
    - Description: An unseen sinkhole collapses underfoot, creating a sudden drop.
    - Challenge: Characters must make Reflex saves to avoid falling in. Those who fall must make Climb or Acrobatics checks to escape.

##### Ruins
1.  **Crumbling Watchtower**
    - Description: A tall, stone watchtower with walls covered in ivy and a partially collapsed roof.
    - Treasure: An ancient, rusted sword with an insignia of a forgotten order.
    - Monster: A nest of giant spiders lurking in the tower’s basement.
2. **Sunken Temple**
    - Description: A half-submerged temple in a swampy area, with weathered statues of unknown deities.
    - Treasure: A hidden chamber containing a stash of gold coins and ritualistic artifacts.
    - Monster: A territorial water elemental that guards the temple’s secrets.
3. **Deserted Village** 
    - Description: A collection of small, dilapidated huts surrounding a dried-up well.
    - Treasure: A locked chest buried beneath the floorboards of the village elder’s house.
    - Monster:A pack of feral dogs that have taken over the village. Eerie whispers can be heard at night, hinting at a ghostly presence.
4. **Forgotten Library**
    - Description: A moss-covered building filled with rotting shelves and crumbling books.
    - Treasure: A single intact tome detailing powerful, forgotten spells.
    - Monster: None, but some of the books are cursed and could harm the reader.
5. **Forgotten Burial Mound**
	- Description: An overgrown burial mound with a stone entrance, half-hidden by the roots of an ancient tree.
	- Treasure: A set of ornate burial jewelry and a finely crafted spear.
	- Monster: A restless undead guardian, protecting the mound’s treasures.
6. **Ancient Forge**
	- Description: The remains of an old forge, with a broken anvil and scattered tools.
	- Treasure: A hidden compartment in the forge containing rare, enchanted ingots.
	- Monster: A small god dwells in the forge, seeking to relight its fires.

##### Natural Locations

1. **The Whispering Glade**
   - **Description**: A serene forest clearing surrounded by ancient, towering trees whose leaves rustle with an eerie whisper, even in the stillest air.
   - **Features**: The whispers convey messages from the spirits of the forest, offering cryptic guidance or warnings. Hidden beneath a particularly old tree lies a buried cache of ancient coins and relics. Beware of the forest spirits that might not take kindly to intruders.

2. **The Crystal Caverns**
   - **Description**: An expansive underground cave system filled with shimmering crystal formations that glow faintly in the dark.
   - **Features**: The crystals resonate with a strange energy, and certain formations can reveal glimpses of the past. However, the caverns are home to territorial subterranean creatures, making exploration perilous.

3. **The Floating Island**
   - **Description**: A large chunk of rock and earth that levitates several hundred feet above the ground, held aloft by unknown magical forces.
   - **Features**: Accessible only by flight or a series of treacherous, crumbling steps carved into the mountainside, the island is covered in lush vegetation and rare herbs with potent medicinal properties. The ruins of an ancient mage’s laboratory lie at its center, haunted by spectral guardians.

4. **The Singing Falls**
   - **Description**: A majestic waterfall that cascades down from a high cliff, producing a hauntingly beautiful melody as the water strikes the rocks below.
   - **Features**: The sound has a calming effect on all who hear it, soothing both mind and body. Hidden behind the falls is a secret cave, home to a small, glittering hoard of gemstones and a powerful enchanted artifact. The cave is also the lair of a dangerous water serpent.

5. **The Enchanted Meadow**
   - **Description**: A vibrant, flower-filled meadow that seems to be perpetually bathed in the golden light of dawn.
   - **Features**: The flowers here have various properties, including healing and enhancing senses. The meadow is watched over by a nature spirit who may curse those who disturb the peace. Rumors say that those who fall asleep in the meadow never wake up.

6. **The Shadowed Gorge**
   - **Description**: A deep, narrow ravine shrouded in perpetual twilight, where shadows dance and flicker even without a light source.
   - **Features**: The gorge is filled with rare, bioluminescent fungi and hidden pools that reflect the night sky. It is also home to elusive, shadowy creatures that guard a hidden library of ancient, forbidden knowledge inscribed on stone tablets. Explorers often lose their way and never return.

##### Inhabited Locations

1. **The Hermit's Hollow**
   - A secluded cave hidden behind a waterfall, home to an old hermit who offers cryptic advice and potions made from rare herbs.

2. **Fey Glade**
   - A small clearing in the forest where a group of fey creatures dwell, their presence marked by mysterious lights and playful illusions.

3. **The Witch's Cottage**
   - A tiny, crooked house deep in the woods, where a solitary witch lives, brewing potions and casting spells in exchange for rare ingredients or interesting tales.

4. **The Bandit's Hideout**
   - A concealed camp within a dense thicket, occupied by a small band of outlaws who might trade stolen goods or offer information for a price.

5. **The Druid's Grove**
   - A sacred grove tended by a solitary druid who communes with nature and provides healing or guidance to those who respect the forest.

6. **The Underdark Entrance**
   - A hidden entrance to a network of underground tunnels, inhabited by a small community of reclusive and wary underdark dwellers who have limited contact with the surface world.




#### Weather events
1. **Blizzard**
    - Description: A fierce snowstorm with heavy snowfall, strong winds, and near-zero visibility.
    - Effect: Travel grinds to a halt, and there's a high risk of getting lost or separated from the group. Exposure to the cold can cause frostbite or hypothermia.
2. **Torrential Rain**
    - Description: Heavy, continuous rain that floods low-lying areas and turns paths into muddy quagmires.
    - Effect: Travel is slowed as the ground becomes slippery and treacherous. Flooded streams and rivers may become impassable, forcing detours.
3. **Mountain Windstorm**
    - Description: Sudden, powerful gusts of wind that sweep through mountain passes and valleys.
    - Effect: Strong winds can knock travelers off their feet, push them dangerously close to cliffs, and make it nearly impossible to set up tents or light fires.
4. **Thunderstorm**
    - Description: A violent storm with thunder, lightning, and heavy rain.
    - Effect: Lightning poses a direct danger, especially in open areas or under tall trees. The loud thunder can also spook animals and cause rockslides in mountainous regions.
5. **Dense Fog**
    - Description: Thick fog that reduces visibility to a few feet.
    - Effect: Navigation becomes extremely difficult, increasing the risk of getting lost or separated from the group. The damp air also chills travelers and makes it hard to stay dry.
6. **Heatwave**
    - Description: Unseasonably high temperatures that last for several days.
    - Effect: The heat saps energy and can cause dehydration or heatstroke. Finding water and shade becomes critical, and travel during the hottest part of the day is dangerous.


#### Travellers 
1. **Treasure Hunters**
    - Description: A group of rugged adventurers armed with maps, shovels, and a variety of weapons.
    - Motivation: Seeking out ancient ruins and rare artifacts within The Maw, hoping for a big score.
    - Interaction: They may offer information on recent discoveries or sell maps to supposed treasure sites but can be secretive and distrustful.
2. **Monster Hunters** 
    - Description: Heavily armed and armored individuals or small teams, carrying an assortment of traps and monster parts.
    - Motivation: Hunting rare and dangerous creatures within The Maw for their valuable parts, used in crafting and alchemy.
    - Interaction: They might seek to trade monster parts for supplies or offer bounties for specific creatures.
3. **Miners**
    - Description: Tough, dirty, and tired individuals carrying picks, shovels, and sacks of ore.
    - Motivation: Heading to or from the mines near Durinson's Rest, looking for precious metals and gems.
    - Interaction: Miners can provide insights into the local terrain, share tales of danger, or request protection in exchange for information on hidden veins of valuable ore.
4. Miners 
5. **Scholars**
    - Description: A small group of academic types, equipped with notebooks, strange instruments, and a couple of guards for protection.
    - Motivation: Studying the ancient ruins and unique flora and fauna of The Maw, seeking knowledge and historical records.
    - Interaction: Scholars may ask for assistance in finding specific sites or offer to trade knowledge and lore for protection and guidance.
6. **Displaced Settlers** 
    - Description: Families or small groups of weary travelers with wagons loaded with belongings and a few livestock.
    - Motivation: Fleeing from a nearby conflict or natural disaster, looking to resettle in or near Durinson's Rest.
    - Interaction: Settlers might seek help with directions, supplies, or protection, and in return, they can share news from other regions or offer basic trade goods.


#### Small gods or Magical creatures
*Add some ideas to this that are not small gods but just magical creatures or encounters*

1. **A God of Lost Rings**
   - **Domain**: Lost jewelry, specifically rings
   - **Mood**: Melancholy
   - **Encounter**: A tiny, forlorn figure found near a stream, searching through the pebbles. It loves hearing the stories of the rings it finds and will reward those who share such tales with a glint of hidden treasure nearby.

2. **A God of Forgotten Songs by the Old Oak**
   - **Domain**: Songs forgotten by travelers resting under a specific old oak tree
   - **Mood**: Nostalgic
   - **Encounter**: A whispering voice heard when sitting beneath the oak tree. It delights in hearing new songs and will grant a small blessing to those who sing or play music in its presence.

3. **A God of Rainy Afternoons at the Stone Bridge**
   - **Domain**: Gentle rains that fall on the ancient stone bridge
   - **Mood**: Calm
   - **Encounter**: A translucent figure that appears during light rainstorms at the bridge, shedding raindrops. It enjoys quiet company and will bless those who sit peacefully with it during the rain.

4. **A God of Broken Toys Left by the Old Well**
   - **Domain**: Abandoned toys left around a specific old well
   - **Mood**: Lonely
   - **Encounter**: A small, patchwork creature that emerges from the well at night. It loves to fix toys and will help repair any item left for it, gifting a token of gratitude in return.

5. **A God of Whispering Winds by the Cliffside Path**
   - **Domain**: Breezes that whisper secrets along a cliffside path
   - **Mood**: Playful
   - **Encounter**: A dancing, invisible entity felt as a gentle breeze along the path. It enjoys playing games and will reward those who can listen and decipher its whispered secrets.

6. **A God of Abandoned Homes in the Ravine**
   - **Domain**: Forgotten houses in a secluded ravine
   - **Mood**: Reflective
   - **Encounter**: A ghostly figure seen wandering through the ruins. It loves to hear stories of the homes’ past and will share hidden knowledge with those who recount the history of the place.

7. **A God of Morning Dew in the Meadow**
   - **Domain**: Fresh dew that covers the meadow each morning
   - **Mood**: Joyful
   - **Encounter**: A sparkling, dew-covered being that appears at dawn in the meadow. It delights in seeing new growth and will bless travelers who greet the morning dew with respect and care.

8. **A God of Shadows in the Cave Entrance**
   - **Domain**: Shadows that gather at the entrance of a specific cave
   - **Mood**: Mysterious
   - **Encounter**: A shadowy figure that emerges from the cave’s entrance at dusk. It enjoys playing tricks and will reward those who can solve its riddles or navigate its shadows.

9. **A God of Broken Promises at the Crossroads**
   - **Domain**: Unfulfilled vows left at a lonely crossroads
   - **Mood**: Bitter
   - **Encounter**: A sorrowful entity seen at the crossroads at dusk. It seeks closure and will assist those who attempt to mend broken promises, offering a token of reconciliation.

10. **A God of Wildflowers in the Hidden Glade**
    - **Domain**: Wildflowers that bloom in a secret glade
    - **Mood**: Vibrant
    - **Encounter**: A colorful, flower-covered figure that appears at midday in the glade. It loves to see life flourish and will grant a small boon to those who plant and care for flowers within its domain.

11. **A God of Moonlit Nights at the Secluded Lake**
    - **Domain**: Moonlight reflecting on a hidden lake
    - **Mood**: Peaceful
    - **Encounter**: A silver, glowing entity that appears under the full moon by the lake. It enjoys calm and tranquility and will bless those who share a peaceful night with it by the water’s edge.

12. **A God of Forgotten Roads by the Overgrown Path**
    - **Domain**: Abandoned paths overgrown by nature
    - **Mood**: Wistful
    - **Encounter**: A spectral traveler seen walking an overgrown, forgotten path. It finds joy in guiding lost travelers and will help those who honor the path by clearing or walking it with reverence.

13. Salmon of knowledge
	- **Encounter:** A pond or small lake, with a fish that seems oddly unafraid. If they touch or eat the fish, they will gain some knowledge.
