### Opening

- Set the scene
	- Lush description of C.L. Patient Ordinal
		- The sun rises on Ondra and Caster Lemma Patient Ordinal, a mobile camp of the Adamant Ratio, here in the north
		- These camps are the equivalent of ships, but their pronouns are xe/xyr
		- Like all Ratio locations, it emulates the spiral, starting from an inner stone chamber with a heavy wooden door, and a stockade wall one iteration deep forming the semi-square walls of the camp
		- The spiral continues from there, in the form of stone markers, and lighter defensive installations, among which more tent encampments, and patrolling Ratio soldiers
		- But it's not only the Ratio's most devout disciples here: the Ratio accepts refugees and visitors, so long as they submit to the magical screening - and work for their stay after 3 days
		- We have
			- Rufus: a loyal soldier of the fifth iteration - the Ratio's version of rank
			- Niko: a refugee who struggled into camp a couple weeks ago, but has earned the trust of the camp as assistant to the camp chef
			- "Ishmael": an annual visitor to the camp and member of the esteemed travelling college, who's giant wombats pulled the college's caravan away just a couple days ago
			- Beitris: a young nomad, and ward of {Kirk's}, who joined the travelling college's caravan a few weeks back, and now stays on to continue journeying with Ishmael
		- Besides these colourful visitors, the camp is normally dour, serious, focused.
			- PROMPT: What's do you see around the camp?
				- Start with Stevie 
				- Maybe show the map
				- Mention the broken ballista
		- Today, there is a nervous, even celebratory air. Tomorrow, the new origin will be sanctified.
		- Tomorrow, CL Patient Ordinal will end its mobile life, and be transformed into a permanent settlement
		- It's a rare thing, but it shows the strategic importance of this position along trade routes - especially the value of ores, relics, and knowledge flowing southward from Durinson's Rest
		- To signify this, a new origin stone will be placed at the center of camp, in the inner chamber
		- They call it a stone, but it's a precious, magically imbued cone, made of a mixture of mithril and adamantine. It begins the spiral that forms the boundaries and lifeways of this camp, and offers a durable source of magical power to the camp's operations... and defenses

### Nightsong

- The sun goes down, and not long after, you take your sleep
- Then, as you always have, since you were a child, no matter what culture you were raised in, you rise at Nightsong
- Var hangs in the sky
- Here at the camp, it's a time of storytelling, and even some light music and drums
- Similar gatherings are happening outside the camp
- A couple of Ratio guards do a sort of sword dance - it's a play telling a famous heroic tragedy from their history - but Kirk's character knows its much older than that
- As Var slips from its apogee, some people start wandering off to bed
	- If they are getting suspicious and watching, DC20 to notice someone with a drum who heads towards the origin and not the bunks

### Initial encounter - after Nightsong

#### Setup 

- It's the evening and some of the non-Ratio camp dwellers are gathered around the fire
- There's a bit of a drum circle
- There's a shaggy druid person playing an interesting rhythm
- He gets up and starts dancing weirdly

- DC18 Perception check to notice a rumbling under the fire

- That's when a [[razorworm]] burst forth up through the middle of the fire
- DC20 reflex save on 2d6 damage (1d6 bludgeoning and 1d6 fire)

- It's now dark
- Roll initiative

- Introduce the clock
	- they can call for help as a free action on their turn but it advances the clock
	- every even number round advances it
	- if the razorworm breaks a a wall, it advances it
	- any major setback advances it
