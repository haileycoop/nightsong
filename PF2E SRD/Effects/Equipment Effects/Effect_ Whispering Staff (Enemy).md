# Effect: Whispering Staff (Enemy)
![[icons-weapons-staves-staff-simple-spiral-brown.webp|150 lp right]]

Through the staff's strange whispering, you gain a glimpse into the mind and desires of one creature you can see within 30 feet. Until the end of your next turn, that creature is [[Off-Guard]] to you and takes a –2 circumstance penalty to saving throws against your spells.
