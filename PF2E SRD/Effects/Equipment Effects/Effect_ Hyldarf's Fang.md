# Effect: Hyldarf's Fang
![[systems-pf2e-icons-equipment-weapons-specific-magic-weapons-hyldarfs-fang.webp|150 lp right]]

Granted by [[Hyldarf's Fang]]

You call upon Hyldarf's power to gain the magic of a linnorm for a brief time. For 1 minute, you gain the effects of _[[Fly]]_, _[[Unfettered Movement]]_, and _[[Truesight]]_ as well as fire resistance 20.
